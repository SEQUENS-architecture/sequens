# SEQUENS MATHS SPEC (v1) - held until generation is live

This is the maths fill of the spine. It sits under `engine-system-prompt.md` and every
rule here is a rule the maths validator enforces in code, not a suggestion in a prompt.
Source: James's gold-standard Year 6 maths prompt, reconciled with the locked SEQUENS
rules. Where the two met, the locked rule wins and the maths rule bends to fit it.

## The one that decides everything: White Rose

1. **Align to the step order, generate original teaching inside it.** White Rose small
   steps run in a fixed sequence, and each step has its own fluency, then reasoning, then
   problem-solving arc. That order is not ours to reinvent. The engine is given the White
   Rose step (from the library, the teacher selects it, its place in the sequence is
   fixed) and builds an original lesson around it in that order.
2. **Never ingest White Rose's own materials.** Not their PDFs, not their slides, not
   their worked examples, whoever else does. The engine generates its own slides that
   teach the same step. `curriculum.awarding_body` stays `null`; the objective is worked
   from as given; an `honest_note` records that the sequence follows White Rose and the
   teaching is original. This is a hard line, and on a product being sold it is the line
   that protects the whole integrity claim.
3. The teacher's OWN materials are uploadable and may be held and built around. Only
   published-scheme materials stay out.

## Maps onto the spine (no second lesson structure)

The gold-standard prompt and the six-part spine are the same pedagogy. They map cleanly,
and the maths lesson uses the spine, not a parallel flow:

- Flashback 4 and Do Now  -> `first_thing`
- Teacher input, I Do      -> `show_them_how`
- Guided practice, We Do    -> `build_it_together`
- Mid-lesson checkpoint     -> `quick_check`  (the one hinge)
- Independent practice, You Do -> `their_turn`
- Exit ticket               -> `last_thing`

## Maths hard rules the validator enforces

1. **Flashback 4.** `first_thing` carries exactly four retrieval questions: last lesson,
   last week, last term, arithmetic. Each has an answer and a named likely misconception.
   Cold-call prompts vary lesson to lesson.
2. **Three steps to success, taught not decorative.** Exactly three, child-friendly,
   displayed, and woven through: modelled in `show_them_how` ("Step 1, I..."), prompted in
   `build_it_together` ("Which step are we on?"), self-checked in `their_turn`, and
   assessed by the exit ticket in `last_thing`. A lesson whose exit ticket does not test
   the steps fails.
3. **I Do, We Do, You Do explicit.** Modelling with worked examples and dual coding in
   `show_them_how`; questioning and scaffolded joint work in `build_it_together`;
   independent work in `their_turn`.
4. **Independent work progresses fluency -> reasoning -> problem solving,** in that order,
   White Rose workbook style, with a Going further strand that is deeper reasoning,
   alternative methods, proving answers. Going further is offered to all, allocated to
   none (locked rule).
5. **One hinge, on `quick_check`,** naming the misconception it catches and why the wrong
   answer is diagnostic. The exit ticket in `last_thing` is separate and may carry its two
   questions (one fluency, one reasoning) linked to the objective and the three steps. The
   hinge is the checkpoint that drives the tracker; the exit ticket is the close. Both
   exist; they are not the same field.
6. **Adaptive teaching by rotation, teacher-facing only.** The teacher-focus group and the
   TA-support group rotate daily across prior-attainment groupings. This logic lives ONLY
   in `teacher_key`. No pupil-facing ability label ever appears (locked rule): not on a
   slide, not in a pupil instruction. Barriers are named by barrier, not by child.
7. **Misconceptions are identified and addressed explicitly** in `show_them_how` and at
   the checkpoint.
8. **Nothing vague.** Timings, teacher script where it helps, board-layout notes,
   questions to ask, expected pupil responses, embedded differentiation in the layers. A
   non-specialist can deliver it with no further planning. Vague filler fails.

## Output

Same contract as the base prompt: one lesson JSON object, passed unedited to the
validator, renders only if every rule above and every spine rule holds. Slides,
worksheets and visual models are generated by code from that JSON (`render.js`), never
hand-built, never lifted from a scheme.
